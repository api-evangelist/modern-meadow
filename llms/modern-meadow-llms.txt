# Modern Meadow

> Modern Meadow is a bio-design and materials-science company (founded 2011, Brooklyn; now
> Nutley / Pine Brook, New Jersey) that uses synthetic biology and protein engineering to make
> animal-free, bio-fabricated alternatives to leather. Its flagship material, INNOVERA, is made
> from plant proteins, biopolymers and recycled rubber with more than 80% renewable carbon
> content, and is sold into interiors, fashion and accessories, footwear, and automotive.

## What Modern Meadow is

Modern Meadow is a materials manufacturer, not a software vendor. It has **no public API, no
developer portal, no API documentation, no SDKs, no OpenAPI/GraphQL/AsyncAPI/MCP surface, no
`/.well-known/` documents, and no GitHub organization.** Contract discovery on 2026-08-01
probed every candidate path on both of its hosts and found nothing machine-readable.

- Founded 2011 in Brooklyn by Andras Forgacs, Francoise Marga, Gabor Forgacs and Karoly Jakab
- Innovation lab in Nutley, New Jersey (opened 2017); HQ Pine Brook, New Jersey
- Roughly $236M raised
- Technology lineage: Bio-Alloy platform -> BIO-VERA (2023) -> INNOVERA (2025, current flagship)
- Partners: BASF; HELLER-LEDER (2026 European joint venture); Allbirds (Terralux footwear line)
- Industries: interiors, fashion and accessories, footwear, automotive
